💲 CostPerPrompt — Prices & method

Public list prices, USD per 1M tokens, checked Oct 2026. Token counts are a heuristic estimate (±10–15%), not a provider tokenizer. Costs assume a reply of ~2× prompt length until a real reply is visible — use for model comparison, not billing.

The model behind this math is tokenecon: cost = in_tokens/1e6 × in_price + out_tokens/1e6 × out_price. The 28× spread on the same job is the point — choosing the model is the biggest cost lever.

To update prices, edit pricing.js and reload the extension at chrome://extensions.
